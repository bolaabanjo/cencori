**Crypto credit top-ups are live.**

You can now add credits to your Cencori account using USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, or TRX — alongside the existing card payments via Polar.

## How It Works

Head to your [billing dashboard](/dashboard/organizations) and toggle from **Card** to **Crypto**. Select a credit pack and you'll be redirected to a hosted checkout where you can pay with any supported crypto asset.

Once the transaction confirms on-chain, credits land in your account immediately — no manual approval, no support ticket.

## Supported Assets

| Asset | Chains |
|-------|--------|
| USDC | Ethereum, Solana, BNB, TRON |
| USDT | Ethereum, Solana, BNB, TRON |
| BTC | Bitcoin |
| ETH | Ethereum |
| SOL | Solana |
| BNB | BNB |
| TRX | TRON |

## Pricing

The same credit packs — $10, $25, $50, $100, $250, $500 — are available in crypto at a 6.5% fee (1% processing + 5.5% platform). No unexpected spreads or hidden surcharges.

## Why Crypto

Some teams prefer crypto for treasury management. Some operate in regions where card payments are unreliable. Some just want the option.

You don't need to choose one or the other. Both payment methods coexist on the same account. Credits are credits regardless of how you fund them.

## Get Started

Visit your [organization billing page](/dashboard/organizations), switch the toggle to Crypto, and pick a pack.
